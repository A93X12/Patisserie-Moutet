Notre Histoire

Two Worlds, One Artisanal Table

Opened in December 2022 by pastry chef Gaël Moutet and his wife Sara, Pâtisserie Moutet was born from a simple devotion: honoring the precision of French haute pâtisserie while weaving in subtle flavors from Gaël’s international journey across Asia.

Every pastry is 100% handmade on-site in limited daily quantities. By gently reducing sugar and working with seasonal Riviera harvests—such as local citrus and strawberries from La Gaude—alongside Valrhona chocolate and zero artificial colorings, each creation lets pure ingredients speak for themselves.

Inside our bright, minimalist boutique, Sara welcomes guests with warmth and freshly baked savory quiches, turning a brief stop in Old Town into a memorable ritual.

Les Incontournables

A glimpse of our rotating daily selection

Signatures d'Orient

Matcha roll cake, yuzu-raspberry-matcha choux, pineapple pangkour, and seasonal entremets infused with rose, lychee, or pistachio.

Classiques Français

Regional Côte d'Azur lemon tart, Madagascar vanilla flan beauvaisien, tiramisu & chocolate éclairs, chestnut tart, and Valrhona madeleines.

Viennoiserie & Salé

Flaky croissants, kouign-amann, Italian pistachio brioche, apple or pear-chocolate chaussons, house pain de mie, and Sara’s warm daily quiches.

Chocolats & Glaces Maison

Tasting chocolates with Japanese Uji matcha, gyokuro, and hojicha, alongside small-batch seasonal ice creams in pandan, hojicha, and asam boi.

“Because every batch is crafted in limited numbers each morning, we invite you to visit early before our daily creations sell out.”
